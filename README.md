# The Vacuum's Alibi

The line (94 characters):

> The crumb is in witness protection. I can neither confirm nor deny the existence of that spot.

A robot vacuum's alibi, deadpan and bureaucratic: the crumb it missed is under
protection, so the vacuum refuses to admit the spot exists at all. The field
was full of load-bearing crumbs and lidar reclassifications; this one takes a
fresh angle — the spot has legal protection, and the vacuum pleads the fifth.
